ProcrastiNOT

A student planner that makes a realistic, explainable recovery plan when a fixed commitment disrupts a study day.

Approved by learner

The distinctive idea

When plans change, ProcrastiNOT replans around fixed commitments, completed work, deadlines, available study time, and protected sleep. It explains its decisions—and says when a deadline is at risk—instead of pretending everything fits.

One disruption, a clear recovery

The student stays in control: the schedule engine produces a proposal to review, not a mysterious or unavoidable change.

What moved?Before-and-after plans highlight rescheduled study sessions.
Why did it move?Short explanations describe scheduling choices.
Can everything fit?Deadlines at risk are flagged honestly.

A deliberate proof-of-concept boundary

The first version focuses on one reliable, editable sample-day recovery—not a complete student productivity suite.

Now The rescheduling loop
  • Editable sample-day classes and commitments, study sessions, tasks, deadlines, priorities, available time, and protected sleep.
  • Add or edit tasks and commitments; trigger a predictable surprise lab.
  • Use deterministic scheduling to preserve fixed commitments and completed work, fit study into available time where possible, and flag deadline risk.
  • Compare the original and proposed plans, understand the changes, and review and approve the proposal.
Later Broader student-planner features
  • Full weekly or multi-day planning.
  • Sticky notes, reminders, a study timer, and AI-assisted study materials.
  • Authentication, other nonessential capabilities, and more developed study-buddy features.
Cut Not a substitute for the core proof
  • A generic to-do list or chatbot instead of schedule recovery.
  • AI/API-generated scheduling decisions; the MVP's decisions must be deterministic and explainable.
  • A visual-only mock-up with no actual schedule recalculation.
